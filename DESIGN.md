## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the regal serenity, artisanal depth, and refined grace of traditional Indian high couture, specifically handcrafted Kanjivaram and heritage handlooms. It bridges centuries-old temple weaving traditions with contemporary minimalist editorial luxury. 

The aesthetic is anchored in warm ivory architectural canvases, balanced by deep ceremonial crimson, regal jewel undertones, and warm metallic accents. The interface feels like an archival art monograph: uncluttered, deliberate, and reverent of the textile craftsmanship. Generous breathing room, precise hairline rules, and high-contrast typography evoke poise, quiet confidence, and tactile authenticity.

The emotional experience is contemplative and dignified. Visual clutter is eliminated to direct all focus to the intricate zari weaves, authentic textures, and artisanal stories of each garment.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from heritage temple architecture, ceremonial vermilion, woven zari threads, and unbleached tussar silks:

- **Primary (`#7A1C2C` - Temple Crimson):** Used deliberately for key focal points, primary interactive states, luxury markers, and curated collection callouts. A deeper maroon variation (`#5C1224`) is reserved for pressed states and critical luxury signifiers.
- **Secondary (`#C5A059` - Antique Muted Gold):** Represents authentic zari threadwork. Reserved strictly for refined filigree dividers, subtle craftsmanship badges, delicate border trims, and quiet foil accents. Never used for long-form reading.
- **Tertiary (`#0D2F4F` - Peacock Royal Blue):** An aristocratic jewel tone employed selectively for secondary tags, heritage provenance badges (such as GI tags or handloom certifications), and bespoke concierge CTAs. Emerald green (`#11382B`) serves as an auxiliary accent for seasonal curation tags.
- **Neutral & Canvas (`#FAF7F2` - Warm Ivory & `#F4EFEB` - Raw Silk Cream):** Forms the foundational ground of the experience. It offers a warm, gallery-like ambience far softer and more tactile than sterile optic white.
- **Typography & Structural Text (`#1A1817` - Deep Noir & `#2D2A28` - Charcoal Bark):** Delivers uncompromising editorial contrast and legibility while preserving the organic warmth of the ivory plane. Subtle muted labels use `#6E675F`.

## Typography

The typographical hierarchy mirrors a high-end luxury editorial lookbook. It contrasts the grand, literary curves of `Playfair Display` against the crisp, open geometry of `Plus Jakarta Sans`.

- **Editorial Headings (`Playfair Display`):** Used for brand declarations, collection chapters, and poetic storytelling. It conveys historical weight and couture authenticity. Italic variants are reserved for artisanal quotes, provenance regions (e.g., *Kanchipuram*, *Varanasi*), and collection descriptors.
- **Utilitarian & Metadata (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Generously tracked in all uppercase treatments (`label-sm`, `label-md`) for navigation links, micro-labels, pricing, and button labels. This ensures effortless scannability while sustaining an airy, modern, and poised composure.
- **Numbers & Currency:** Rendered in `Plus Jakarta Sans` with tabular or proportional lining figures to ensure pure clarity when displaying luxury price tiers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an unhurried, classical 12-column editorial grid designed to showcase vertical imagery without crowding.

- **Canvas Architecture:** Maximum content container width is capped at 1440px for editorial catalog views and 1600px for full-bleed hero spreads. Generous outer margins (`margin-desktop: 4rem`) frame the products like physical exhibition prints.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Sections are separated by sweeping vertical buffers (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`). This pace mimics moving through private salon chambers, letting each collection breathe before the user encounters the next.
- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, 1.25rem outer margins, 1.5rem vertical rhythm. Product catalogs transition to single-column or an alternating 1-column / asymmetric 2-column rhythm.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout, 2.5rem outer margins, 1.5rem gutters. Product listings resolve to balanced 2-column grids.
  - **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout, 4rem outer margins, 2rem gutters. Product grids feature 3-column layouts with generous vertical aspect ratios.

## Elevation & Depth

In keeping with refined editorial fashion sensibilities, the design system avoids heavy drop shadows and artificial skeuomorphic layering. Instead, depth is established through tonal layering, precise hairline borders, and ambient light dissipation:

- **Tonal Stepping:** Layering is communicated by placing warmer secondary surfaces (`#F4EFEB`) over the primary ivory canvas (`#FAF7F2`), framing imagery and bespoke modules without artificial lift.
- **Filigree Boundaries:** 1px hairline strokes in antique gold (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.35)`) or soft noir tint (`rgba(26, 24, 23, 0.08)`) create delicate, architectural boundaries between modules, navigation ribbons, and product compartments.
- **Ambient Veil Shadows:** For floating elements—such as bridal styling concierge drawers, quick-view viewports, and interactive mega-menus—elevation is conveyed via subtle, warm-tinted diffusion:
  - *Floating Layer:* `0px 12px 32px -4px rgba(26, 24, 23, 0.06), 0px 4px 12px -2px rgba(122, 28, 44, 0.03)`
  - *Contextual Modals:* `0px 24px 64px -8px rgba(26, 24, 23, 0.12), 0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.05)`

## Shapes

The shape philosophy is understated and architectural. A soft roundedness tier (`1`) is applied with strict restraint:

- **Standard Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Small Badges):** Feature an ultra-subtle corner radius of `0.25rem` (4px). This preserves the sharp, structured poise of luxury print editorial while avoiding harsh, unfinished corners.
- **Overlays, Drawers & Cards:** Default to `0.25rem` or crisp `0px` outer silhouettes. The 3:4 and 2:3 vertical product viewports remain razor-sharp rectilinear planes to honor the authentic geometry of folded handloom silks.
- **Pill Exceptions:** Restricted solely to floating filter pills or circular floating icon triggers (such as audio guides or wishlist pins), where geometric circularity denotes instant utility.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Temple Crimson):** Solid `#7A1C2C` fill, pure warm ivory text (`#FAF7F2`), `0.25rem` border radius, uppercase tracked typography (`label-md`). Hover state gently shifts to `#5C1224` with a smooth 200ms ease.
- **Secondary / Bridal Concierge Action:** Transparent background with a crisp 1px border in `#C5A059` (Antique Gold) or `#7A1C2C`, styled with uppercase typography. Hover introduces an ultra-soft gold or crimson wash (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.08)`).
- **Text / Editorial Link:** Deep charcoal text with a permanent or hover-revealed 1px hairline underline offset by 4px, evoking high-fashion catalog index listings.

### Product Cards
- **Aspect Ratio:** Fixed 3:4 or 2:3 vertical ratios, framing models from head to floor to display drape, border zari, and pallu craftsmanship.
- **Interaction:** On hover, the image scales smoothly (scale 1.03 over 700ms cubic-bezier(0.25, 1, 0.5, 1)) accompanied by an optional secondary reveal displaying textile weave close-ups.
- **Details Section:** Positioned beneath the imagery with generous vertical clearance (`space-md`). Contains category in tracked `label-sm`, title in `headline-sm`, and price in tabular `body-md`.
- **Wishlist Pin:** Floating delicate antique gold heart/pin outline situated top-right, enclosed in a translucent ivory circle (`rgba(250, 247, 242, 0.85)`).

### Craftsmanship & Provenance Badges
- **Visuals:** Minimalist inline or overlaid pill badges featuring muted gold or peacock royal blue backgrounds (`#0D2F4F` at 10% opacity) with solid metallic typography (`label-sm`).
- **Use Case:** "Pure Zari Certified", "Kanchipuram Silk Mark", "Single-Weaver Handloom", and "Bespoke Bridal".

### Form Controls & Inputs
- **Text Fields:** Minimalist single-line underline inputs with 1px border in `rgba(26, 24, 23, 0.2)` transitioning to solid `#7A1C2C` upon active focus. Floating labels set in `Plus Jakarta Sans` gracefully shift to uppercase `label-sm`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Handcrafted square or circular borders in `#C5A059`, filled with `#7A1C2C` when checked.

### Content Dividers & Filigree
- **Section Breaks:** Subtle horizontal hairline rules (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.3)`) punctuated by a centered diamond glyph (`◆`) or floral zari motif to separate catalog chapters and designer narratives.
