Anatomy of an Heirloom

Every authentic Kanjivaram consists of three distinct woven entities united through sacred technique.

01

The Body (Udal)

Spun from double-warp mulberry silk filaments, imparting the signature crisp weight, tactile strength, and structured drape unique to Kanchipuram.

02

The Interlocked Border (Korvai)

Hallmark

Woven with three distinct shuttles where contrasting border wefts lock seamlessly into body warp fibers, forming the revered jagged temple spire joints.

03

The Grand Pallu (Mundhi)

Integrated through the meticulous 'Petni' technique, fusing separate gold zari warp filaments into the silk body for breathtaking ceremonial opulence.

"Two weavers, three shuttles, four weeks of uninterrupted devotion. The Petni technique binds contrasting silks to create an unbroken drape that lasts generations."
— Murugesan, 4th Generation Master Weaver Guild Patron
